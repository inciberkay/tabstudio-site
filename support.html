TabMotion Support

Questions, problems or a Guitar Pro file that does not open correctly? Open an issue at github.com/inciberkay/tabstudio-site/issues and describe what happened. Please do not attach files you do not have the rights to share.

Common questions

Which files can I open?

Guitar Pro files (.gp). Some timing features are not supported yet, such as tempo ramps, grace notes, fermatas, segno or coda jumps, and swing. TabMotion tells you when a file uses one of them instead of guessing.

Which format should I export?

ProRes 4444 keeps the background transparent and is widely supported by video editors such as Final Cut Pro, Premiere Pro and DaVinci Resolve. PNG sequences are transparent too. MP4 needs a background color.

How do the free exports work?

Your first 3 completed exports are free, with every feature. Cancelled or failed exports do not count. After that, playback, scrubbing and Export need the one-time full version purchase. Opening Tabs, changing settings and the still preview stay free.

I bought the full version on another Mac.

Open the purchase window and choose Restore purchases while signed in with the same Apple Account.
