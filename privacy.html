TabMotion Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 4, 2026

TabMotion does not collect, sell or share personal data. The developer receives no information about you or how you use the app.

Your files

Guitar Pro files, reference images and exported videos are opened, processed and saved only on your Mac. They are never uploaded.

Free export count

To count the 3 free exports, TabMotion stores a single number on your Mac, in your keychain and in your own iCloud account (iCloud key-value storage). It contains no personal information, and the developer cannot read it.

Purchases

Purchases and restores are handled by Apple through the App Store. TabMotion only learns whether the full version is owned. Apple's privacy policy applies to payment information.

Analytics and tracking

TabMotion contains no analytics, advertising or tracking, and makes no network requests of its own.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Contact

Privacy questions: open an issue at github.com/inciberkay/tabstudio-site/issues.
